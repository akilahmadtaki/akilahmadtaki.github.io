Diffractive Networks Learn Imagers, not Inverses

A. A. Taki, S. A. Fattah, M. Saquib

Under review at IEEE ICASSP 2027

We show that diffractive optical networks trained for reconstruction through random phase diffusers learn generic imaging operators rather than diffuser-specific inverses, using trainable phase-only layers and differentiable wave propagation.

ProxMatch: Feature Space Proximity for Calibrated Pseudo-Label Propagation in Semi-Supervised Learning

A. A. Taki, W. Aspen, F. Suya

Under review

Unlabeled data points should be selected for training only when they lie close to labeled points in feature space, as proximity indicates higher prediction confidence. We use this principle to calibrate pseudo-label propagation in semi-supervised learning.

LOOPE: Learnable Optimal Patch Order in Positional Embeddings for Vision Transformers

M. A. M. Chowdhury, M. R. U. Rahman, A. A. Taki

CVPR Findings, 2026

OpenReview
We propose a novel method to determine optimal patch ordering for spatial position embeddings in Vision Transformers, ensuring minimal loss of positional information while maintaining computational efficiency. We also introduce a controlled experiment using minimal pixel configurations to analyse the behaviour of different positional encodings.

RBMatch: Dual-Level Class Rebalancing for Semi-Supervised Building Footprint Extraction

A. A. Taki, S. A. Fattah

Under review at IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (GRSL)

A class-rebalancing self-training framework for semi-supervised building footprint extraction, combining adaptive thresholding, confidence-aware loss reweighting, and distribution alignment at both pixel and image levels.

Low-Fidelity Synthetic MRI Degrades Rare-Class Classification in Diffusion-Based Augmentation

A. A. Taki, M. I. H. Bhuiyan

Under review at ICCIT 2026

We show that low-fidelity synthetic MRI generated by few-shot adapted diffusion models degrades rare-class classification performance, with LoRA-adapted Stable Diffusion failing to capture MRI global intensity structure that a from-scratch diffusion model resolves.
