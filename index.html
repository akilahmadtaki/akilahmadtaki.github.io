Hello! I am a M.Sc. student in Communication & Signal Processing at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), currently maintaining a CGPA of 4.00/4.00. I am also a Lecturer in the Department of EEE at the University of Asia Pacific. My research interests broadly include Computer Vision, Applied Machine Learning / Deep Learning, Large Language Models, and Wireless Communication. I am working under the supervision of Prof. Shaikh Anowarul Fattah at BUET, and collaborating remotely with Dr. Fnu Suya at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.

I completed my B.Sc. in EEE from BUET with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00. During my undergrad, I worked on brain MRI lesion segmentation using attention-gated fusion networks. I am passionate about bridging the gap between deep learning research and real-world applications in medical imaging, vision transformers, and next-generation wireless systems.
